What mobcorp.cc actually is today

Status note

mobcorp.cc is the holding-company concept for the MobCorp portfolio — an AI orchestration platform and central allocator for the ventures beneath it. That orchestration backend doesn't exist yet; this page won't claim otherwise.

The one real, live, monetized product at this domain is a company-filings search tool (SEC EDGAR full-text search) with a free tier and a $4 30-day Pro tier, gated by a real Stripe checkout. Try it on the live domain.
